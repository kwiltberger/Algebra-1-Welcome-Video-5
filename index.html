How to Complete a Self-Check and Extra Practice

Check your understanding, strengthen weak skills, and decide whether you are ready to move forward.

Purpose of the Page

The Self-Check helps you make a plan.

🔍 Notice Identify which skills you understand and which skills still need work.
🧠 Review Return to examples and explanations when something is still unclear.
🎯 Prepare Decide whether you are ready for Prove It! or need more support first.

Confidence Ratings

Rate yourself honestly for each skill.

🔴 Still Learning I need another explanation or more guided practice.
🟡 Getting There I understand part of the skill but still make mistakes.
🟢 Confident I can complete the skill and explain my reasoning.
1

Attempt Each Question First

Solve the self-check question on your own before opening the answer or explanation.

Read Identify what the question is asking.
Solve Write your steps and make your best attempt.
Check Open the answer only after you finish.
The answer check is meant to help you learn. It is not a shortcut for skipping the problem.

Compare Your Thinking

Study the explanation, not only the final answer.

If Your Answer Is Correct

Compare your method with the explanation and make sure your reasoning is complete.

If Your Answer Is Incorrect

Find the step where your work changed, review the related Learn It section, and try again.

🎮 Extra Practice
2

Complete the Extra-Practice Activity

Use the activity to review important concepts and strengthen skills that are not yet confident.

  • Read the directions before starting.
  • Complete the full activity.
  • Review incorrect matches or answers.
  • Repeat the activity when more practice is needed.

Make a Readiness Decision

Use your results to choose what happens next.

➡️ Ready Continue to Prove It! when you can complete and explain the weekly skills.
↩️ Review Return to Learn It! or Practice It! when you need another example.
📅 Get Help Schedule a Teacher Check-In when questions are still unresolved.
